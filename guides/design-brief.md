# Design brief

Fill this in (in the app's `DESIGN.md`) **before** writing UI code. Keep it to half a page.

- **Subject, audience, primary job:** who uses it, where (phone at the gym? desk?), and the one
  thing it must do well.
- **Screens:** each screen and its **one** primary action.
- **Tone:** three adjectives, plus one or two real-world references (a product, a print style).
- **Palette:** 4–6 named colours with hex values, light and dark. Text contrast is at least 4.5:1.
- **Type:** 1–2 families and a size scale. Lines stay under about 70 characters.
- **Layout and density:** mobile first at 360px, then what changes on desktop.
- **The one memorable element:** spend boldness in one place; keep the rest quiet.
- **States:** empty, loading, error, and offline if needed. Each has real copy that says what to
  do next.

## Avoid (the generic defaults)

- Inter or Arial with no other choice, purple-to-blue gradients, and cream with terracotta.
- Identical rounded cards everywhere, with a shadow on everything.
- Tiny uppercase "eyebrow" labels, numbered 01/02/03 sections that aren't a real sequence, and
  emoji as icons.
- Everything centred, and hero sections taller than their content.
- Vague buttons ("Submit", "Click here"). Name the action: "Save exercise".
